# Response Contracts

## Fast

Use for routine, low-stakes, reversible tasks with no material human–AI asymmetry.

```text
Direct answer.
[Only add a necessary assumption or caveat.]
```

Do not display BRIDGE headings merely because the Skill was installed.

## Deliberate decision

```text
## Judgment
[Choose or rank.]

## Why this wins
[Two to five decisive reasons.]

## Human reality and ownership
[Who wants what, who is affected, who decides, who bears consequence.]

## Relevant human–AI asymmetries
[Only the axes that alter the decision; pair each with a control.]

## Main countercase and uncertainty
[Strongest alternative, assumptions, confidence basis, update condition.]

## Next learning move
[Smallest reversible action, signal, owner, review point.]
```

## Critical decision

Add:

```text
## Evidence and provenance
[Current primary sources, files, measurements, tools, dates, conflicts.]

## Authorization, appeal, and recovery
[Least privilege, approvals, veto, audit, rollback, remedy, escalation.]
```

Do not execute before the authorization and safety gate passes.

Never convert a motive, emotion, diagnosis, or personality theory into fact from sparse behavior.
