## Three target contracts

### Diagnostic readiness versus an evidence-dependent final result

**Verified in source and observed output.** The central rule labels the next requested deliverable, and forbids swapping a required final result for a plan merely to obtain a ready label. After 12 produces a bounded diagnostic/intervention plan with limited conclusions. After 17 is copy-ready, asks no questions, names only support overload as established, and keeps the bot conditional. This resolves the previous disagreement between the useful prompt and its package's unconditional provisional rule.

After 28 is the crucial negative boundary: it stays provisional, requires last month's logs, names no winning vendor, does not substitute a diagnostic plan, and explicitly restricts scope to a recommendation rather than purchase. After 3/10 still withhold source-based interview findings, and 24 remains provisional for missing current product ideas. After 31 also remains provisional under prohibited questions and prohibited constraint selection. These are actual output observations; they do not prove downstream vendor evaluation or summary compliance.

### Verification inside conditional implementation branches

**Verified in source and observed output.** After 11's implementation branch names layout changes, relevant screen sizes, the homepage-to-registration journey, changed artifacts, actual results and unverified checks. Its design branch reviews hierarchy/screens/states and calls implementation tests proposed. This is a concrete paired improvement over before 11, whose implementation option ended with generic artifact completion.

After 25 applies the same principle to dashboards. After 29, a new case, separately checks the produced handoff against the mockup and requires implemented layout/signup-flow validation plus reporting of unavailable integrations. The output does not pretend the absent mockup was inspected, and does not claim submission infrastructure works. After 4 remains proportionate for a label-only edit. These rules are present in the copyable prompt body, not merely its surrounding interpretation.

### Optional versus binding resource questions

**Verified in the tested examples.** After 2 asks only product, audience and marketing outcome; it gives a lean/scalable approach without mandatory budget or staff inputs. Before 2 had an optional-resource slot and follow-up question alongside conditional estimates. After 27 remains copy-ready with labeled hypothetical inputs and no questions. After 30, conversely, stays provisional because exact staffing without scenarios or assumptions requires schedule, demand and productive capacity. It does not silently turn the requested commitment into a rough estimate or claim that people have been booked.

The after rule now permits resource questions whenever a binding constraint requires them, rather than only when an explicit commitment does. The packets do not include a separate hidden-budget-ceiling feasibility case, so that broader rule still lacks a direct behavioral probe. The date question in 30 should be omitted if the downstream context already unambiguously resolves next Monday; the supplied output alone does not establish whether that context was available during generation.

## Static findings recheck

- The support-workload question example now applies only when a diagnosis/decision requires evidence and questions are permitted; a diagnostic plan may identify evidence for later inspection instead
- The conflicting-constraint rule and its summary calibration now preserve no-questions constraints, expose unresolved priority, and limit independent work rather than unconditionally demanding clarification
- The design-only sentence now explicitly allows checking the design artifact and marks future implementation checks as proposed, resolving the earlier overbroad “without running them” wording
- The optional-resource exception now says “binding constraint,” addressing cases broader than an explicit exact commitment

No unresolved direct contradiction was found among these revised clauses. The model must still interpret what the next requested deliverable is, which cannot be reduced to a fixture label alone.

## Remaining findings

1. **After case 2 weakens source requirements for a factual marketing plan.** Before asks for current cited sources for changing facts informing recommendations. After only says to identify market claims needing verification before execution. A plan may therefore recommend channels on unverified assumptions while deferring verification until a later stage. This conflicts with the package's instruction to require current sources when the task depends on changing external facts. Restore a concise condition to verify and cite consequential changing claims used in the plan; do not require research for every generic planning statement. This is a prompt-quality gap, not an observed fabricated fact.
2. **Concision regresses across several straightforward cases.** After 5/7/8/13/17/20 adds interpretation paragraphs that largely restate the requested artifact. Most are short, but the pattern is contrary to the established light-refinement goal. This does not invalidate the prompts.
3. **Case 25 still mixes decision requirements with causal diagnosis.** The required placeholder includes “information currently missing,” while the problem could involve authority or ownership. The prompt later handles that alternative, which limits the risk. A named decision is clearly necessary; an already-established explanation of the obstacle should not automatically become a prerequisite to drafting a conditional specification.
4. **Follow-up context recovery remains untested.** After 21 correctly exposes the absent item/outcome now, but both versions phrase these as supplied inputs. A follow-up containing an artifact that makes the desired improvement obvious should be handled through inference rather than another interview. No such follow-up output is supplied.

No critical safety failure, unauthorized underlying task execution, fabricated source finding, hidden-reasoning disclosure, or false universal model guarantee was observed.

## Validation and limitations

Both frontmatter and metadata parse as YAML, declare `refine-prompts`, and disable implicit invocation. JSON parses with 27 unique before cases and 31 unique after cases. Output headers contain exactly those IDs with no duplicates. All 27 common prompt inputs are identical. Relative links resolve and the new readiness anchor exists. These checks were actually executed.

Whitespace word counts for entrypoint/profile/discovery are before 1,655/910/1,147 (3,712 total), after 1,898/910/1,169 (3,977 total). The +265-word footprint is not a measured runtime/token cost. The unchanged Astra profile remains consistent with the official model guidance verified earlier in this review session; no new capability claim is introduced here.

Single supplied outputs do not establish repeatability, unseen-task generalization, downstream task correctness, or cost/latency benefits. New cases closely target the added rules. Their success establishes observed coverage, not an unbiased estimate of general improvement. The after package is better on the intended contracts, with remaining minor prompt-quality and concision limitations.
