# Quality gates

## Synthetic truth

Run fixed-seed cases covering bright, dark, saturated, low-saturation, noisy, and gently graded backgrounds. Include hard edges, antialiasing, thin structures, concavities, enclosed background pockets, semitransparent edges, and foreground colors close to the background. Measure IoU, precision, recall, F1, alpha MAE, and symmetric boundary error. Calibrate mandatory thresholds from observed baselines and retain a degradation case that must fail.

The 2026-08-15 fixed-seed baseline, measured before freezing the gates, produced worst-case IoU 0.968525, precision 0.983542, recall 0.980954, F1 0.984011, alpha MAE 0.016340, and symmetric boundary error 1.868632 pixels. The mandatory gates retain measurable margin without crossing the observed baseline: IoU at least 0.965, precision at least 0.980, recall at least 0.980, F1 at least 0.980, alpha MAE at most 0.018, and boundary error at most 2.0 pixels. Recalibrate only after an intentional fixture or algorithm revision and preserve the prior measurement in version control.

## Watermark review

Analyze every decoded frame after display rotation. Build foreground masks from the supported near-solid background, retain the dominant connected component as the subject, ignore separated antialias or subject fragments within 12 working pixels of its real pixel contour, and group genuinely remote components across time into candidate regions. Add the reported safety padding needed to include low-alpha resampling and motion-blur edges. Report region bounds, padding, first and last frame, frame coverage, and area relative to the subject.

`clear` means no candidate survived this detector; it does not prove that the video contains no watermark. Human review remains required. For authorized removal, require a reviewed display-pixel region and reject dominant-subject overlap. Use background-constrained inpainting away from the canvas edge; use a subject-free same-frame donor with local background-gradient transfer and a smooth outer collar when the region touches an edge. Repeat the detector on every selected repaired frame. Any remaining or ambiguous candidate intersecting a reviewed region fails the run. Preserve candidates outside reviewed regions as diagnostics because an intentional detached prop or separated subject component can resemble a watermark.

## Real animation structure

Measure each frame's boundary residue, suspicious transparent holes, retained enclosed background, foreground area, bounding box, partial-alpha width, transparent RGB, and edge contamination. Keep edge-connected background admission as the default. Permit global admission only with visual evidence that the subject palette does not overlap the declared backgrounds; in global mode, pixels within the declared tolerance are fully transparent anywhere on the canvas. Remove disconnected visible cutout islands only when they are more than two working pixels from the dominant character component; retain near-edge antialiasing. After final resizing, clear sub-16-alpha haze outside a two-pixel support around the retained character core so invisible bridges cannot preserve remote chroma specks. Within the outer four-working-pixel contour, measure visible pixels whose Lab distance to any admitted background color is at most 8.0; fail a frame when their ratio exceeds 0.12. This gate catches coherent chroma spill that structural alpha checks miss. Compare adjacent alpha masks and the last-to-first seam. Detect outliers from median and MAD; report exact frame indices and metrics. These checks establish structural consistency, not pixel-level truth.

Require both a six-scaled-MAD robust outlier and a material difference before rejecting a pair. The measured materiality floors are 0.015 mean alpha difference, 0.040 contour-change ratio, and 0.015 foreground-area change. This prevents sub-percent source-pose changes from being mislabeled as matte instability while preserving rejection of visible jumps. Always include the last-to-first pair in the same gate.

When explicit cadence collapse is enabled, compare every adjacent pair of high-resolution cutout alpha masks before outlining. Collapse a pair only when mean alpha difference is at most 0.003, contour-change ratio is at most 0.012, and foreground-area change is at most 0.003. Preserve non-collapsed motion steps even when only one metric exceeds its threshold. Recompute the groups during verification from hashed cutouts and the selected-cycle timing record; require source and output durations to match exactly within serialized floating-point precision. Run the normal adjacent-pair and loop-seam gate again on the collapsed output sequence.

## Visual evidence

Create cycle-candidate, contact-sheet, alpha, edge-detail, black, white, gray, checkerboard, and loop-seam views. Include the original video and `analysis/watermark.json` in human review, and inspect the repaired region throughout the loop after removal. Report `human_visual_review: pending` until a person explicitly accepts them.

For strongly varying backgrounds, review edge-detail and black, white, gray, and checkerboard composites after the original-background matte. Machine success is invalid if the formal run omitted a visible source-background family. When production consistency is requested, require an outline reference and report the estimated reference color, applied color, and perceptual distance.
